# SEO Audit — gbibec.id (GBI Baranangsiang Evening Church)

**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Data sources (all live/real, not estimates):** live `sitemap.xml` (74 URLs), `robots.txt`, GSC **URL Inspection** per-URL (74), GSC **Search Analytics** (28d), **GA4** (28d, property 527564078), and the Next.js source.
**Site type:** Local non-profit church (Bandung, id-ID). Goal = local discovery + participation, not revenue.

---

## Executive Summary

**Overall health: strong.** This is a young (~5-month), technically clean, richly structured site — not a broken one. **66 of 74 URLs are indexed (89%)**; the `site:` "~30" was a collapsed/estimated display, not the real number.

The ceiling here is **authority + content position + local presence**, not technical fixes.

**Top priorities**
1. **Google Business Profile / local SEO** — the single biggest lever for a local church; brand + "jadwal ibadah" + "gereja baranangsiang" queries already dominate demand.
2. **Convert existing impressions** — sermon posts rank page-1-bottom (e.g. "khotbah minggu 19 juli 2026": 504 impr, pos 8.5, **1 click**). Optimize titles → climb toward top-3.
3. **Fix dilution + orphans** — 24 near-duplicate weekly *undangan* posts; 2 orphaned URLs Google can't find.

**Quick wins:** trim long kabar titles (<60 chars), Request-Index the 8 stragglers, `noindex` or enrich thin form pages, apex→www as 301/308.

## 1. Crawlability & Indexation

### Strong (no action)
- **robots.txt** clean: `/`, key sections Allowed; `/admin /api /promo /sorotan /presentasi` Disallowed (and those carry `robots: noindex` in code too — consistent). Sitemap referenced.
- **Canonicals** self-referencing, consistent `www`, and **match Google's chosen canonical** (verified per-URL via URL Inspection: `canonicalGoogle = https://www.gbibec.id/...`).
- **Index coverage: 66/74 (89%)**, 0 errors.

### Findings

**1.1 — 8 URLs not indexed** · Impact: Low–Med · Evidence: URL Inspection
| Reason | Count | URLs | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| URL unknown to Google (orphaned) | 2 | `baptisan-air-9-mei`, `catatan-khotbah-…-26-april` | No internal links → never discovered |
| Discovered – not indexed | 5 | `formulir/mclass`, `undangan-…-26-april`, `rumah-doa-online`, `ibadah-raya-…-19-april`, `catatan-khotbah-…-12-april` | Old/thin, low crawl priority |
| Crawled – not indexed | 1 | `kom/300` | Similar to other KOM levels; selective, likely temporary |

**Fix:** internal-link the 2 orphans from `/kabar` + related posts; Request Indexing in GSC for the substantive ones (`baptisan-air`, `catatan-khotbah` posts, `kom/300`). Leave truly old invitations.

**1.2 — apex → www is 307 (temporary), not 301/308** · Impact: Low · Evidence: `curl -I https://gbibec.id` → `307`
**Fix:** set the domain redirect to permanent (308/301) in Vercel. Canonicals already point to `www`, so consolidation happens regardless — low urgency.

## 3. On-Page Optimization

### Strong
- **Titles** unique, keyword-first, brand suffix `| GBI BEC`, good length on core pages (36–53 chars).
- **Meta descriptions** unique, keyword-rich, benefit-led (~150–160), localized ("Bandung", "17:00 WIB", landmarks).

### Findings

**3.1 — Some kabar titles exceed ~60 chars → SERP truncation + weak CTR** · Impact: Med · Evidence:
`"Melihat dan MENGERTI — Catatan Khotbah Ibadah Raya 19 Juli 2026 | GBI BEC"` = **75 chars**. That post pulled **504 impressions at pos 8.5 but only 1 click** — the truncated, brand-heavy title is part of the low CTR.
**Fix:** front-load the searchable hook (date + theme + speaker), drop/shorten brand:
`"Khotbah 19 Juli 2026: Melihat & Mengerti — Ps. Owen Sandjoto"` (~58 chars).

**3.2 — Form pages are thin: no H1, no schema, brandless title** · Impact: Low · Evidence: `/formulir/mclass` → H1 = 0, JSON-LD = none, title `"Formulir Pendaftaran M-Class"`; also not indexed.
**Fix:** these are conversion endpoints, not search targets. Prefer **`noindex` the `/formulir/*` set** (keep the info pages `/mclass`, `/baptisan`, etc. as the SEO landings), or add a descriptive H1 + 1–2 intro sentences if you want them indexed. Right now they dilute crawl for no ranking upside.

---

## 4. Content Quality

### Strong
- **Sermon summaries** (17 `catatan-khotbah` posts): unique, substantive, `Article` schema — the real SEO asset, already earning impressions.

### Findings

**4.1 — 24 near-duplicate weekly "undangan" posts** · Impact: Med (site-wide quality signal + crawl) · Evidence: sitemap; weekly invitations on a near-identical template.
Most are indexed, but scaled thin/near-duplicate content can dampen the site-wide helpful-content signal.
**Fix:** consolidate weekly invitations into **one recurring "Jadwal Ibadah" page**, or `noindex` the pure-invitation posts (keep them live for users). Keep every sermon summary.

**4.2 — Local content & Google Business Profile** · Impact: **HIGH** (local church) · Evidence: demand is overwhelmingly local-brand — `gbi baranangsiang` (315 impr, pos 3.1), `jadwal ibadah gbi baranangsiang`, `gereja baranangsiang`, `gbi sukawarna baranangsiang`.
**Fix:** fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (service times, photos, posts, Q&A, category), and keep NAP consistent with the on-site `Church`/`PostalAddress` schema (already present — good). This is the highest-ROI move for a local congregation.

---

## 5. Authority & Links

- Young domain: **brand terms rank well (pos ~3)**; non-brand sermon terms sit page-1-bottom (pos 8–10) — an authority/position gap, not an indexing one.
- **2 orphaned URLs** (no internal links) → "URL unknown to Google". Fix via internal linking (see 1.1).
- Backlinks not audited. For a church, prioritize: a link from the **GBI Sukawarna** parent site, local/denominational directories, community listings.
